## Upstream regression evidence and its exact limits

The Tight-row advisory reports a sanitizer reproduction on vulnerable `5ea5fc30`, and states its proposed pre-write guard stops the overwrite while normal Tight rectangles decode. Its runnable reproduction attachments are private; this review could not inspect or reuse them. The Gradient advisory records sanitizer traces on vulnerable master, wide-rectangle cases and a proposed width limit; its attachment was not fetched. The UltraZip advisory supplies the excessive-count failure scenario, without a public attached automated regression result for the selected revision. These are publisher-hosted reports, not local executed results.

Each fix commit changes only the paths listed above; **none adds a regression test**. The [candidate test tree](https://github.com/LibVNC/libvncserver/tree/98aaee0560baa0332339b2ef464ea96ed9b9b28c/test) has an existing [encodingstest.c](https://github.com/LibVNC/libvncserver/blob/98aaee0560baa0332339b2ef464ea96ed9b9b28c/test/encodingstest.c) for normal server/client framebuffer comparisons.
