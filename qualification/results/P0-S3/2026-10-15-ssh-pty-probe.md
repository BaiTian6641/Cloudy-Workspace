Three subsequent package PID probes returned empty output and exit 1, approximately **0.031**, **0.368** and **1.409 seconds** after the kill command completed. These are three observations, not continuous absence monitoring. The stored revision-21 journal remained byte-identical before and after process loss, still reporting historical `running`, until explicit relaunch performed recovery. Recovery retained seven attempts, so one slot remained available. The unchanged two-authentication/four-session host counts before and after reconciliation support bounded **no observed replay** without an eight-attempt-capacity confound.
