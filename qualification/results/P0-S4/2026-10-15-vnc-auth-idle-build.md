Those source boundaries support a premature client-idle-budget diagnosis and do not turn error 10 into a wrong-password verdict or establish client initialization/frame delivery.

The reviewed post-`rfbClientInitialise` `continuing()` check remains before clearing the authentication deadline or admitting connected work.
